# Liquid Glass back on what floats and on the controls

- Decided by: owner, 2026-11-14
- Status: in force; supersedes [2026-11-09](2026-11-09-liquid-glass-off.md)
- Rule: AGENTS.md › Enforced rules and › Design; DESIGN.md › Surfaces and Controls

Liquid Glass is back only on what floats and on the controls; the page and its panels stay pure black. Each glass blurs what is behind it and nothing else. A control is clear glass with its hairline, a label over art sits on glass with a deeper dim, and what floats (menus, sheets, Ctrl+K, toasts, the player) is black glass over the frost. Rows repeated down a scrolling list take no backdrop, which keeps scrolling cheap, the cost that took glass off on 2026-11-09.
