# Devices

Devices shows what you plug in: your mouse, what you listen on and your controller, each with its picture. Press a card to open its page.

## Mouse

Plug in a supported mouse or its receiver and open its card. Only the settings your mouse has are shown, and every change is written to the mouse and read back. Save a profile and link it to games, and it switches on by itself when one starts. If the mouse isn't found, wake it, check its battery and connection, then open Devices again.

## Headphones

**Pick** the pair you wear, then set up Lucid, Game Mode and Night Shift. Lucid's **Set up** says what it will do first.

## Controller

**Test** checks its sticks and **Measure** its polling rate. A DualSense on a cable can **Calibrate**, with **Undo**. **Lights and sound** sets its light bar, microphone, speaker and vibration, and **Game profiles** change them per game. Exo never changes a controller while a game runs.

The **USB map** button on Devices' title line shows every USB controller and what hangs from it.
