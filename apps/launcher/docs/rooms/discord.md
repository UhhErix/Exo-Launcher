# Discord

The Discord room is Exo's own Discord client: your friends, DMs, servers and calls, drawn by Exo. There is no Discord app to install, and Exo never sees your password.

## Start here

1. Open the room. A QR code shows.
2. On your phone, open Discord, go to **You > Scan QR code**, scan it and confirm.

- **Now** shows the voice rooms your friends are in (with **Join**), who is playing or listening, and what's unread.
- **The list** on the left holds Now, Friends, your conversations and your servers. A server opens its channels under its row.
- **Calls.** Click a voice channel, or the call button in a DM. **Share Your Screen** picks an app or a screen, with its sound.
- **Settings** for Discord live in **Settings > Discord**: Voice and video, Chat, Notifications, Privacy, Account and Keybinds.

## Before you use it

Discord's terms don't allow other clients, and Discord may lock an account it flags. Exo does nothing on its own and keeps one connection open to stay low-risk, but using it is your choice. [Discord accounts](../HELP.md#discord-accounts) says what Exo does and what to do if your account is locked.

More: [Discord in the help](../HELP.md#discord).
