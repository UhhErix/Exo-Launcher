# Exo's rooms

Exo is one window with five rooms centred along the Bar at the top: Library, Store, Discord, Music and PC. **Ctrl+1** to **Ctrl+5** open them in that order, and **Ctrl+K** finds any room, game, action or setting. The gear opens Settings.

| Room | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| [Library](library.md) | Every game from every store in one grid: play, install, update, folders. |
| [Store](store.md) | Browse, search and wishlist games; buy in the store's own app. |
| [Discord](discord.md) | Exo's own Discord client: friends, DMs, servers and calls. |
| [Music](music.md) | Exo's player for Spotify, Apple Music or YouTube Music. |
| [PC](pc.md) | This PC: drivers, display and HDR, Best settings, upscalers, shaders, apps. |
| [Devices](devices.md) | Inside the PC room (the **Devices** button on its This PC line): your mouse, what you listen on and your controller. |

The full guide is [Getting started and help](../HELP.md). To report a problem, go to [getexo.app/help](https://getexo.app/help).
