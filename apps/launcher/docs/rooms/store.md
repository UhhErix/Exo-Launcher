# Store

The Store is where you find new games. Exo sells nothing: **Get on Steam** and the other buttons open the store's own app (or its web page when the app isn't installed), and you buy there.

## Start here

- **Discover** has the shelves: top sellers, coming soon, on sale and the most wishlisted, without the games you already own.
- **Calendar** lists the games coming out.
- **Wishlist** keeps the games you saved; the ones on sale show first.
- **Search the store** finds any game. A game you already own, on any store, shows **Library** instead, which opens it in your Library.

Prices are in your Windows region's currency, and a game's system requirements are checked against your PC.

## If something's wrong

If the Store says it's unavailable, check your connection and press the refresh button on Discover.

More: [Store in the help](../HELP.md#store).
