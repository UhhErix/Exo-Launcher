# Music

The Music room is Exo's own player for Spotify, Apple Music or YouTube Music. The service runs out of sight; Exo draws home, search, your library, queue and lyrics.

## Start here

1. Pick your service in **Settings > Music**.
2. Press **Sign in** in the Music room and sign in on the service's own page.

Each service needs its paid plan: Spotify Premium, YouTube Music Premium or an Apple Music subscription.

- **Songs**, under Home, lists every song you saved.
- **The mini player** in the top bar controls playback from every room.
- **Spotify** also has Jam, Friend activity and your devices. A Spotify link in Discord opens here.

If the Spotify app is running when Exo starts Spotify's player, Exo closes it, because it keeps the player from loading.

## If something's wrong

If the room stays empty or signed out, check the service works in your browser, then sign in again from the Music room.

More: [Music in the help](../HELP.md#music).
