# PC

The PC room is this PC on one page: what it's made of and what keeps it fast. Nothing here changes your PC until you press it, and the changes that carry risk have **Undo**.

## Start here

- **This PC** lists your processor, graphics, memory, screen, Windows and motherboard. **Best settings** sets Exo's power plan, driver profile, network and drive settings, and HDR where your PC has it.
- **Updates** has your graphics driver, Windows and game runtimes (Visual C++, DirectX and the rest). Driver updates wait while a game runs.
- **Graphics** has your driver profile and **Upscalers**, which update the DLSS, FSR and XeSS files a game already has. Close the game first; **Restore** puts the original back.
- **Shaders** precompiles your games' shaders: **Refresh** finds what to compile and **Compile** works through the queue.
- **Display**, **Network**, **Performance**, **Drives** and **Apps** each have their own section. Press your screen for Display and HDR, or your motherboard for the BIOS, Secure Boot and the TPM.

## Opt-in

- **Frame generation in games without anti-cheat** (in Best settings) is off until you turn it on.
- **Auto tune** (a GeForce or a Radeon) is off until you press it, warns you first and goes back to stock by itself if anything goes wrong.

Exo never changes a game with anti-cheat, apart from the vendor's own upscaler file when you allow it for that game.
