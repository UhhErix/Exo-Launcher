# Library

The Library is every game you own, from every store, as one grid of covers. Steam, Epic, GOG, Riot and the other stores stay installed and do the work out of sight.

## Start here

- **Play or install.** Click a game to open its page, or right-click it for Play, Install and Open folder. **Continue** at the top keeps the games you're playing one click away.
- **Downloads and updates** show on the game's card. **Settings > Downloads** sets the hours they run and pauses them while you play.
- **Folders.** **More > New folder**: name it, pick a color and tick its games. A game can be in as many folders as you like, and a folder is how you keep favorites.
- **Properties.** The sliders button on an installed game's page (or right-click its card) has launch options, language, betas, checking files, moving to another drive, DLC and **Artwork** for your own cover. Anything that downloads or deletes asks first.

## If something's wrong

- **A game is missing.** Press **More > Refresh library**. For Epic, GOG or Amazon, sign in under **Settings > Library**. For a game outside any store, press **More > Add a game** and pick its .exe.
- **A store's servers are down.** Exo says so once and offers **Play offline** for Steam.
- **Hide a game** with right-click **Hide**; the sort menu's **Show > Hidden games** brings it back.
