# Design

Exo is judged on five things. Every visual decision answers to one of them.

1. **Immersive.** The game's art is the picture; Exo is the frame. Chrome stays out of the way, and art gets the light.
2. **Clean, and comfortable.** Nothing on screen that is not doing a job: no taglines, no ledes, no eyebrow labels, no explaining the obvious, no decoration. Facts people come for are a job: hours, storage, achievements, a price, a drive's free space, whose playlist it is. They stay, easy to read, with room to breathe. The test for any line: would someone want to see this at a glance? Warm and generous, never bare.
3. **Modern.** Rounded, quiet, fast. Motion is welcome anywhere it is done right: it shows work happening, change landing or things arriving, answers at once and settles (Exo's two curves), never bounces or adds shadows, and under Reduce motion only fades.
4. **Only backgrounds are the page.** The page, each room's background, is Void black (`#000000`); panels and cards on it are black with the one hairline (Surfaces), and what floats is Liquid Glass (owner, 2026-11-14): sheets, dialogs, menus, popovers, Ctrl+K, notices, a room's side panels, Music's player, and every control. Glass blurs what is behind it and nothing else, and one appearance serves every screen. One hairline sets a surface apart; no word on any surface drops below its contrast, and cards inside a panel are clear with the hairline, neither gray nor black holes in it.
5. **Consistent.** One vocabulary of rows, pills, hairlines and states, used the same way in every room.

## Exo's language

Exo takes what makes Apple's whole family great (its systems and its apps: Music, TV, App Store, Games, Photos, Maps, Weather, Fitness) and builds its own from it, not a copy of any one screen. Five rules, everywhere:

1. **Content is the interface.** Art is the biggest thing on every screen; chrome is small, quiet and the same in every room. The page itself is one flat color (pure black): no glow, no tint.
2. **One bar, for what applies everywhere.** The top band holds **Search** on the left (the Exo mark, the one way to find anything; Ctrl+K), the **Rooms** centered between the live capsules, and **Tools** on the right (Capture, Settings, then the window controls after a hairline, all in one capsule). A room's own controls (sort, Wishlist, the Store's search) stay in the room, in the Bar's rhythm. The bar shares its width and compacts in steps on smaller windows; nothing overlaps.
3. **Live beside the rooms.** What is happening right now has its own capsule beside the rooms (Dynamic Island, Live Activities): the call on the left, the song and the mouse on the right. Each has a slot of its own and slides in and out there, so the tabs never move. The Exo bar is Exo's progress (Motion › Progress).
4. **Float means one thing.** Controls are black with their hairline; panels, sheets, menus, popovers and notices are near-black slabs with the same hairline; panels are 18px, cards 14px, sheets 24px. What sits on a slab (its controls, a menu or dialog opened in it) is a lift of the slab itself, never a second slab inside it.
5. **Feel.** Every press gives way a little (0.97, 120ms); things that change size morph (220ms); pages fade (160ms); nothing waits on the network to draw (cache first).

## Grammar

- **Rows.** Label left, control on a shared right edge, one hairline above. At least 62px in Settings and setup, 42px where space is tight.
- **Facts.** What people open a page for gets a size of its own, not a muted run-on line: its value at 20 Medium in full ink with its name under it at 12 Muted, spaced 40px apart and wrapping (the game page), or a value at 13 Medium with a detail line under it at 13 Muted (PC's parts). A dotted line carries two or three short facts on a card or plate (hours and last played on Continue), not five. A fact Exo doesn't know is left out, never a dash. What is wrong sits under the numbers in Warn or Bad; it never takes their place. Each fact comes from data the page already holds; a row never makes a host call of its own.
- **Meters.** `components/ui/Meter.tsx`: a still 4px track, the ink up to the value, for how full something is now (a drive), with words beside it saying how much. It has no sheen and no motion, so it never reads as work under way (Motion › Waits has the bar for that).
- **Covers.** A Library cover stays bare at rest, the art alone. Pointed at or focused, one glass plate at its foot gives its name, hours and storage; only that one is drawn, so a grid never pays for a backdrop per card. Continue's plate says hours, last played and Update ready. A Store card says its price or date and what players think; Shaders' poster its name and state.
- **Pills.** Segmented choices are bare words, the current one a white pill with black words; the group has no outline of its own (never a pill inside a pill). Used for music service, permissions, store chips, sort.
- **Controls.** Small Liquid Glass: clear (`--exo-fill`) over the glass's blur and tone (`--exo-control-glass`), edged by the one hairline (`--exo-rim`; fields `--exo-field-rim`, the same line). No outline ring. Fields (search, text, the Discord composer) are the same control, their words on the true center; a field's placeholder and icon are Muted, as the quiet icon buttons beside it (Faint is for no word you read). Hover lifts the fill (`--hover-fill`); a press takes the pressed lift (`--exo-rim-pressed` keeps the edge, a touch quieter) and gives way (0.97), with no movement under Reduce motion. Controls repeated down a scrolling list (a song's More, a friend's Message) are a lift of the page instead: on black they look the same and cost nothing per row. On a slab (a menu, a dialog) a control is a lift of that slab. Primary action is the one control in the ink's color (white on Void). 28px compact, 32px standard, 44px primary. One fill per role in every room (Exo Mouse draws the same roles, from the values Exo sends it):

  | Role | Rest | Hover | Pressed |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Primary | `--color-fg`, text `--color-primary-fg` | `--exo-primary-hover` | the same, giving way (0.97) |
  | Raised (secondary, chips, fields, segment groups, the Bar's outlined capsules) | `--exo-fill` with `--exo-rim` (fields `--exo-field-rim`) | `--hover-fill` | `--exo-pressed`, `--exo-rim-pressed` |
  | Quiet (icon buttons, tabs, menu rows) | transparent | `--hover-fill` | `--exo-pressed` |
  | Danger | `--exo-fill`, text `--color-bad` | `--hover-fill` | `--exo-pressed` |
  | Current (tab, segment, row, a toggled icon) | `--exo-selected` | | |

  No outline ring on a control and no flat gray pill: a raised control is glass with its hairline, a quiet one bare. No rim or halo on a primary. `exo/control-fill` fails a control whose fill is a literal gray.

  One set of buttons, in every room: `.exo-set-btn` for every button (32px, 13px Medium; `.is-primary`, `.is-danger`, `.is-compact` 28px), `<Button variant="primary">` for a page's lone primary (first run, Setup, a signed-out room), `<Button variant="ghost">` for a quiet one (the Bar's icons, Back); the game sheet's Play is its 44px `.exo-play`. A choice of a few is one segmented pill (`.exo-shelf-chips` holding `.exo-shelf-chip`s, the current one `aria-pressed`), an on/off is `components/Switch.tsx`; never a native checkbox or radio. ESLint fails anything else.
- **Sliders.** One: `components/ui/Slider.tsx`, drawn once in `ui/src/styles/chrome.css` (Exo Mouse copies it value for value). A 4px track filled white up to the value over `--exo-track`, a 14px white knob; compact (3px, 10px, the knob under the pointer) in the Bar, vertical for the equalizer, bare over a track of its own (Discord's input sensitivity, whose live level is the track). `exo/one-slider` fails a range drawn anywhere else.
- **Art.** The wrapper is the art's one rounded clip: no second clip inside it, no fill under the picture (a placeholder shows only with no picture) and no ring, border or inset shadow on its edge, which shows only in the antialiased corner as a ragged rim. Focus, selected and playing rings are an outline 2px outside the art (hover lifts a cover instead: Motion › Hover). `exo/art-edge` fails a ring on an art selector.
- **States.** A word on the right edge in muted gray; green only when something is genuinely on or running. Errors read in `Bad` directly under the control that caused them.
- **Detail lines.** A settings row's `detail` is one short plain line for a value, a result, or what the row does when its name alone doesn't say it (Download hours: "Downloads start only in these hours"). A row clear from its name stays one line. Never a tagline, never a paragraph.
- **Type.** Sentence case everywhere. Controls 13px Medium (500), body 400, labels 10 to 12px Medium; section names are the only bold (600), titles 650. No letter-spacing on lowercase: Exoline spaces itself and already tightens titles, and in Chromium any letter-spacing turns off Inter's contextual forms (the colon in 12:30, the arrow in ->). Small words on a control stay Medium. No tracked capitals, no uppercase labels, no emoji.
- **Hover.** The hover fill (`#1a1a1a`, `--hover-fill`) on a control, 120ms (a menu row lights at once); a game's cover lifts toward you and takes a little light (Motion › Hover). Never a see-through white wash, never a shadow, and a control never scales on hover; a control shown with its row or card fades in and out on the quick time, and a scrim may lift off art. On a slab the hover, current and pressed fills are lifts of the slab itself (glass.css redefines `--hover-fill`, `--exo-selected` and `--exo-pressed` there). Keyboard focus is a 2px white ring; on a slab it sits inside the edge.
- **Size.** Three sizes: 20 for titles, 13 for body and controls, 12 for small text, each `calc(Npx * var(--exo-scale, 1))`. The only others are the page titles (game sheet, Music's pages and the PC room's name 28, first run 32), a Library cover with no art's title (14 to 30px as the card allows, `clamp(14px, 16cqi, 30px)`) and Discord's emoji picker glyphs, listed in `ui/css-design.config.mjs`. A placeholder shows the game's initial only where its name sits beside it (Store, Continue); a Library cover shows the title itself.
- **Panels.** Black with one hairline, 14px as cards; a panel that floats is large glass with the same hairline, 18px; 26/32px padding. A panel's own actions (Update all, Clean up, Use best settings, Select) sit on its title line at the right end, the same in every room (`gpu/SectionHead.tsx`, Apps' group heads); a row's action sits on its row; a form's Cancel and Save at its foot. A settings sheet (Settings, Properties, Discord's user, server and channel settings) is `--exo-settings-w` wide (1160px at 100% text, scaling with it) and 880px tall. Settings has a 232px sidebar of pages (a 20px title, then a 34px row per page, the current one `--exo-selected`); the page it names scrolls beside it under a 20px title, with the close at its top right. Properties and Discord's own settings are one column of sections. A section is a 13px 600 heading 8px above its rows, sections 28px apart, the page padded 26px 32px 32px. Opening one at a section scrolls that section's heading to the column's top. Fill the window; a page that fits without scrolling gets no sidebar and no disclosure.

## System

One look for every room's controls (`ui/src/styles/system.css`, imported after the rooms' own files):

- **Glass on the page.** Buttons, fields, chips and segment groups are small Liquid Glass (Grammar › Controls), what floats is large glass, and panels are black with the hairline; the page under them is flat black. Hover lifts them (`--hover-fill`). Primary is the ink's color.
- **What is chosen is never outlined** (owner, 2026-11-10). A chosen tab, segment or chip is a white pill with black words, as the primary button is, and the glide carries it. A chosen row in a list is a fill (`--exo-selected`) or, in Settings, full ink beside a white marker. A picked picture shows its check, or is the bright one among dimmed ones. A ring is only for keyboard focus and for a live state in its colour (playing, speaking).
- **One line** (owner, 2026-11-10). Every outline, divider and sheet edge is `--exo-line` (#2e2e2e), `--exo-hair` wide: one device pixel (whole pixels from 200%), set by `lib/hairline.ts`. A 1px shadow ring is 1.25 or 1.5 device pixels on a scaled screen and comes out uneven and soft, so no ring states its own width, and none is 1.5px or 2px. No ring inside a ring, no glow round a card, no second grey for a "softer" divider.
- **Floating panels.** A room's sidebar and side panel (Discord's members, Music's Now playing) float as rounded panes of large glass with a hairline (18px), inset `--exo-gap` (10px) from the window and each other, the same as the Bar's capsules, so their edges line up under it. Content stays on the page. Cards in a page take 16px 20px padding. Settings' grouped lists are clear boxes on their pane. A call's dock floats at the Discord room's foot, as wide as the pane above it; the pane ends a gap above it (nothing scrolls under it to show past its edges, no two outlines meet). Through a change of place it holds still with the band.
- **Top bar.** No band. Search and the live capsules (the song, a call, a download, the mouse) are 36px capsules of small Liquid Glass; the rooms and your tools are bare words, the current room a white pill. The window controls close the Tools capsule, after a hairline. The Library starts below the Bar and scrolls inside its own pane, so no cover or word passes behind the Bar's words; every room starts at its lower edge.
- **Switches.** 40×24 with a round white knob; gray off, green on.
- **Sheets** round to 24px; featured art (Continue, the Store's hero), popovers and dialogs to 18px; cards and menus to 14px. Art has no hairline (Grammar › Art).
- **Menus.** One way everywhere (Exo's, Discord's, Exo Mouse's): large glass (Exo Mouse's too, inside its own frame, in the values Exo sends it, with no backdrop blur of its own), 34px rows rounded 8px, the hover lift under the pointer, a check beside the current choice, sections with a quiet heading. A submenu opens beside its row in the top layer; it flips to the other side at the window's edge and keeps its row lit. Anything taller than the window scrolls.
- **Rooms.** Library, Store, Discord (Exo's own client), Music, PC (this PC: its graphics, display, network, drives, performance and apps) and Devices (what you plug in).
- **One layout on every screen.** The page is laid out as on a 1080p screen (in Windows' scaled pixels) and drawn larger on a bigger one (MainWindow.PageScale: Chromium's device emulation, the window's size over the zoom), so a 1440p or 4K screen shows what a 1080p one does, sharper; a 1080p screen or a smaller one is drawn as it is. The zoom brings the screen to 1080p's size either way up (its long side to 1920, its short side to 1080, whichever is less), so a screen stood on end gets the zoom it has lying down and a 16:10 screen keeps 1080p's width; the window's smallest size takes the same zoom.
- **Labels sit on their true center.** Controls draw at Exoline's normal line-height (a taller one draws the label above the middle in WebView2); a small fixed pill (a count, a badge) centers its text in a grid at `line-height: normal`; a group of segments has no border, so its padding alone centers them.

## Surfaces

One appearance: Void black for the page and its panels, Liquid Glass on what floats and on the controls, no light or dark theme. Glass blurs what is behind it and nothing else (`backdrop-filter`, written in `ui/src/styles/glass.css`, its values in `ui/src/tokens.css`). The class names say `glass` too: "small glass" in the code is a control, "large glass" what floats. Every color is a token from `ui/src/tokens.css` (`exo/token-colour`).

- **The page** is Void black.
- **One hairline.** Every outline, divider and sheet edge is the same line (`--exo-line`, one device pixel wide, `--exo-hair`), on a control, a card, a slab and a sheet alike. No lit top, shaded foot, inner glow or sheen: a control casts no shadow, and large glass floats in an even halo (`--glassy-shadow-raised`). The edge is never covered: a sheet that lays art under its words draws its line again above that art. Keyboard focus sits inside the line.
- **A control is black with its hairline** (`--exo-fill`, `--exo-rim`). Hover, current and pressed are lifts of its fill, not new colors.
- **What is chosen is a white pill** with black words (the current room, tab, segment or chip), never an outline or a ring inside a ring; a chosen row is a lift (`--exo-selected`).
- **A label over art sits on a plate** (small glass with its deeper dim, `--exo-control-glass`): Continue's titles, a cover's name, badges.
- **What floats is large glass**: menus, popovers, sheets, Ctrl+K, notices, dialogs, a room's side panels and Music's player are black glass (`--glassy-overlay`, a 36% dim) frosted over what is behind them, lit by the same hairline, over a scrim (`--exo-scrim`, which dims the Bar too) where they cover the room. The hairline separates them; large glass floats in an even halo.
- **The Bar's capsules are glass**, and the Library starts below the Bar (System › Top bar).
- **Legible by contrast.** Words are white; secondary words use the tokens in Tokens. The E2E suite measures every word on the installed app from the screen's own pixels (`rooms` and `appearance`: under 3:1 fails, under 4.5:1 warns; `appearance.solid` checks that no room content runs under the Bar).
- **Light on press.** A press lights a control from within where it lands (`ui/src/lib/glass.ts`, `glass.css`); under Reduce motion it is still.
- **Grain** under the words so dark gradients don't band.

**App icon.** The mark as the repo ships it (`tools/make-logo.py` draws every size, the installer's screens and the web marks from one geometry): the three bars of the E on a dark squircle plate, with detail that follows size: at 32px and under the plate is a plain gradient and the bars flat white on whole pixels, so the taskbar stays crisp. The mark inside the app stays the bare bars in the ink.

**Icons.** Lucide (lucide.dev, ISC, through Iconify's `@iconify-json/lucide`, always the newest release; owner, 2026-11-19), one family everywhere: Exo, Exo Mouse, setup, and the checks that draw themselves in. Minimal outline icons, each the one that fits its action. `ui/scripts/tabler-map.mjs` names the Tabler icon for each of Exo's names, `ui/src/brand/icons.tsx` exports them by those names, and `npm run icons` writes `brand/glyphs.ts` and stops on a name with no icon, an icon nothing names, or two names sharing one icon. One glyph per meaning: two things that mean different things never share one. The line follows the size (`lineAt`): Tabler's 2px at 24, 1.5px at 16. A toggle that is on (liked, starred, pinned) and the current room show Tabler's `-filled` form where it has one. Brand marks (stores, Discord, Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music) stay their own. No emoji, no native `title=` tooltips.

**The Exo Island.** An outlined capsule left of the room tabs, only while something is live that the current room isn't showing: a Discord call outside its room (the server's icon, the channel over the server, the faces in the call with a green ring while one speaks, mute, deafen, leave); downloads show in the Library. The song has its own capsule right of the tabs. Each capsule has a fixed slot, slides down into it when it starts and back up when it ends, so the tabs never move. Nothing live, nothing drawn.

**Achievement banner.** Over the game it is solid Void black with the one line for an edge, no shadow and no glow (`Assets/Overlay/banner.html`, which copies the values it needs): the picture at 64px, the name in white with its tier beside it in one word and one colour (Rare, Epic, Legendary, Complete), what it asked of you, and how far along you are and how few have it. Each tier is a build, a hit and a hold, its hit timed to its thock: common lands; rare settles its picture and runs silver along the edge; epic drops in with weight and ice light both ways; legendary is the picture alone, lifting, until the card opens from it on the hit, with gold light and a gold edge at rest; completion does that over three hits and sends one line out. Transform and opacity only, over in about two seconds, then still until it leaves (5, 6, 7, 9 and 11 s). Under Reduce motion, Exo's own, every tier is a fade.

**Ctrl+K.** One bar for everything: rooms, games, actions, music controls, Discord DMs and servers. Enter does the obvious thing.

## Motion

Motion answers what you did, at once, and settles; it never asks to be watched. Tokens in `ui/src/tokens.css` (Exo Mouse copies them):

- **Two curves.** What appears, fades or changes state eases out (`--ease-out`, `cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)`: fast at once, then settling). What travels between two places or changes shape springs (`--ease-spring`, a `linear()` spring from rest, damped at 0.9, so it never visibly overshoots: 0.15%, a twentieth of a pixel on a 24px move; the same shape at every duration). `linear` is for progress only. No bounce anywhere.
- **Five times.** Press 90ms (`--dur-press`), quick 120ms (a press letting go, a hover leaving, anything closing), base 160ms (menus and popovers appearing, a choice, a tab's content), move 220ms (a selection or knob travelling, a page pushing, a sheet settling), flight 300ms (art flying). Nothing the eye follows runs longer, except a room's first arrival.

The moves:

- **Press.** Every capsule control gives way to 0.97 in 90ms and comes back in 120ms, through the `scale` property so it never fights the control's own transform; a pressed fill darkens with it. A control also lights where it is pressed (90ms in, 300ms out).
- **Hover.** A control's fill comes up in 120ms (the ease-out puts most of it in the first frames). A menu row lights at once, so the row you are on is never half lit, and lets go in 120ms. A control shown with its row or card (a song's Play and More, a capture's star, a banner's close) fades in and out in 120ms, everywhere the same. A game's cover, in the Library, the Store and Shaders, lifts toward you, 3px up, and takes a little light from its own brightness, never a white layer, so its blacks stay black; a scrolling shelf leaves room above for the lift. It never grows: Chromium draws a growing layer soft until it stops, a blur you see on every hover. Nothing else moves on hover; under Reduce motion covers only take the light.
- **Appear.** Slabs (menus, popovers, a popover of fields, a profile card, the key popover) grows from its trigger's side, 0.96 to 1 with its opacity on the base time, and leaves by fading on the quick time; a context menu only fades, in 90ms. Sheets (Settings, Properties, Discord's settings, the Store's game page) settle in from the middle, 0.98 to 1 on the move time, over a scrim that darkens in by its color; the scrim's dim reaches over the Bar too. Ctrl+K grows from 0.98 on the base time and fades out on the quick one. A Bar capsule drops 8px into its slot from 0.96 (fading in on the base time, the drop on the spring), and goes back up fading on the quick time. A notice comes down 8px from under the Bar into the stack and leaves by fading while its slot closes, so the ones below glide up. A sheet or dialog settles by its size alone, its words there from the first frame; a game page or a room comes up from under a black cover that fades away; rows and buttons arrive by a rise alone; and an entrance fade holds only before it starts (`backwards`), never after it ends. Only what floats alone (a menu, a popover) fades itself.
- **Arrive.** Opacity 0 to 1 and an 8px rise, 280ms, 24ms apart, the eighth and later together, so a shelf has landed within half a second. Only the first time a view shows its items; scrolling, sorting or filtering never replays it.
- **Choose.** A selected fill, ring or mark eases in on the base time. A group's one selected fill (the Bar's rooms, chips, segmented choices) travels to the new choice on the spring, moving and resizing as one shape; a switch's knob travels the same way. A toggle's icon fills in as SF Symbols' do: the fill grows from 0.8 inside the line as it comes up (a liked song, a starred capture, the current room's glass body). Someone speaking gets a green ring that comes and goes on the quick time everywhere (the call's members, tiles and faces, the Island, the Bar's call), so a voice flickering on and off never strobes.
- **Switch.** A tab's content is there at once. Pictures crossfade on the base time.
- **Move.** One view becoming another uses the browser's View Transitions (`ui/src/lib/viewTransition.ts`, `ui/src/styles/transitions.css`). A room crossfades as it slides 12px toward the tab you chose: the old one gone in 120ms, the new one in on 160ms, its slide on the spring. A page inside a room (Store's Discover and Wishlist, Music's pages, Discord's places and servers, and settings) pushes 24px in from the side of what you chose, on the spring (back, quicker). Art that opens something (a game card, a Store tile, a cover, a picture, a capture, the mini player's song) flies to where it lands and back on the spring over the flight time, cropped like the picture and keeping its corners, its two pictures crossfading under it. The Bar, Discord's band and Music's player stay live through a move. Presses go through mid-move. Names are set only for the move and only on what is on screen, so the rooms kept mounted never collide.
- **Waits.** Five kinds, each for its own job and the same everywhere it is used; a wait under 300ms shows nothing.
  - *The bar* (`components/ui/Progress.tsx`, `styles/progress.css`), only for work with a measure: a download, an install or update, a shader compile, an upload, a scan that counts. A track (the ink at 16%) holds the ink's fill, softer at its start and lit at its end, with a sheen running through it every 1.6s; the fill slides in from the left on a transform, linear over 220ms as progress is, and nothing of it is ever drawn past the track's ends. A measured step that doesn't know its amount yet glides a lit third across the track every 1.4s. Done turns it green; a stop short turns it red. Small 3px on a card or art, 4px in a row, a notice or a page, large 6px for a window's one bar. Words beside it say what and how much ("Downloading · 2.1 of 40 GB"), never only a percentage. A transfer's card or row carries it along its foot (`edge`).
  - *The spin*: a control whose glyph names its action turns that glyph while it works (Refresh, Retry, Sync: `exo-spin`). A control of words carries Exo's ring (`components/ui/Spinner.tsx`) before its words, which stay ("Saving changes"). Never a bar in a button.
  - *The skeleton* (`components/ui/Wait.tsx`, Skullet, themed in `styles/waits.css`): content on its way is the real thing's own markup with sample words, laid out unseen, and Skullet draws a skeleton in exactly that shape with one sweep of light across the page, so nothing moves when the content takes its place. A sample never fetches or subscribes, and only the wait goes in `Wait`, never the content. No words, no bar, no spinner in the middle of a page.
  - *The shine* (`exo-shine`, `styles/waits.css`): content being refreshed stays and shines where it is (Library's covers, the Store's banner), a soft band of light every 1.6s.
  - *Nothing*: what is already on screen refreshes quietly behind itself (stale while it revalidates); only what changes moves, as it lands.

  Screen readers get the bar as a progressbar with its label and value, the ring as a status with its label. Under Reduce motion nothing turns, glides or slides: a known bar moves to its place at once, an unknown one holds half lit, a shine holds still and a little lighter, a skeleton holds still.
- **Done.** The art dims, a green check draws itself (300ms) and a 1px ring turns green; after about 2.4s the art and ring ease back. Versions or states that changed stay green.
- **Scroll.** No scrollbar, anywhere in Exo or Exo Mouse (the Library's scrubber is an index of its groups, there only while the grid moves or the pointer is at its edge): everything scrolls by wheel, trackpad, keys and touch, and nothing is drawn over what you read. A list that goes on shows it by fading into its pane's edge. The browser's scrollbars are hidden everywhere by one rule (`styles/scrollbar.css`); under forced colors Windows' own scrollbars return for whoever needs them.
- **Keys.** What the keyboard does lands at once: a room or page it changes doesn't slide, a menu or Ctrl+K it opens doesn't grow, a selection it moves jumps (`data-exo-input`, `lib/motionPreference.ts`). Art opened with Enter still flies.
- **Reduce.** Exo's Reduce motion (`data-exo-motion="reduce"`; Windows' animation setting stands in only until Exo's settings load): nothing slides, grows, springs, flies or loops, and presses don't give way. Sheets and their scrims still fade in on the base time, opacity alone, and rooms and pages crossfade, so nothing snaps into being; glass that floats (menus, popovers, Ctrl+K, notices, the Bar's capsules) is there at once; a warning's red still flashes, and a jump to a message stays lit. One list in `exo-shell.css` says what still fades.
- **In the background.** Only loops (the bar's sheen and glide, a spin, a shine, someone's typing dots, the Discord Nitro name effects) and the Store's featured-game timer pause while Exo is not focused; they are listed in `tokens.css`. Entries always finish, so a page opened while a game has focus is never stuck invisible.
- **Lean while playing.** While a game runs or streaming software is open (OBS, Streamlabs, XSplit, PRISM, Meld, Twitch Studio, vMix), Exo steps back by itself, with nothing to set and nothing on screen changing: it yields the processor, releases its memory 15 s after the game takes the front, and holds its own background work. While streaming software is open, Streamer Mode is on by itself: personal details (the Exo account's email, Discord's email, phone and invite links) are hidden, and Discord's notifications and sounds stay quiet (`Services/LeanSignal.cs`).
- **Never.** Bounce, anything spinning but a working control's glyph or ring (Motion › Waits), a control growing on hover, a shadow on hover, a white wash over art, animated layout where a transform would do, or motion that loops while nothing is happening. One shake is kept: Discord's unsaved-changes warning, a no as macOS shakes a wrong password.

A slab, 24px, 400 to 420px wide, over the scrim, the title (20px) and one or two lines (13px) centered, then equal capsules on one row, Cancel first and focused so Enter alone never confirms, and the action named for what it does ("Uninstall", never "OK" or "Yes") last, the primary or in Bad when it deletes; no WinUI accent anywhere in it. **Update on open.** When Exo opens and a newer version is out, a small sheet in What's New's look (420px, no list: the changes are What's New's, after the restart): "Update available", "Exo Launcher 3.17.2" under it, one primary Update now. **Notify.** One stack, the same everywhere (`lib/notify.ts`, `shell/NoticeStack.tsx`): what finished or happened while you weren't pressing anything (a download, an update, a background event, a message), or a removal whose control went away with it (an unpin, with Undo), at the bottom right (above Music's player; at the top right it sat on a room's own sort and More), newest lowest, three on screen at most: a fourth waits its turn (a passing one a newer arrival moves out, a failure, a wait or an Undo is never moved; one that waited past its own time is dropped), so it is never a wall. Each is a slab, 380px, one shape: its glyph in its tone (a check for done, an alert in red for a failure) or a person's picture, a bold title when it is about someone or something with where it happened beside it, quieter, one short line under it (two at most, then an ellipsis), and at most one button at the right (Install, Open, Undo, Repair; one that asks does it in place, `confirm`), then its ×; a wait runs along its foot with the Exo bar. No tagline, no helper text, no second button. A passing one goes after the time its words take to read (the time to notice it, 2 s, and 50ms a character: 4 s at least, 10 s at most; 7 s with a button), held while the pointer or focus is on the stack and while Exo is out of sight, and given its full time again after; a failure, one with a button and one with a wait stay until answered or dismissed. Dismissing is the ×, a swipe to the right (past a third of its width, or a quick flick; it follows the finger and settles back if let go short), or Escape (the one with focus; with focus nowhere else, the newest, unless a menu, a sheet or a field has the key); the × shows under the pointer or focus, and always on one that stays. A notice never takes focus or a press from what you are doing, and costs nothing while none is up (no timer, no listener but the store's). ## Rooms

Each room is one subject, and everything about that subject lives in it, wherever its code sits: a tool's options live with the tool, and Settings is for Exo itself (appearance, downloads, accounts and keys, about). A room never grows a page list for its own sake: what fits one room scrolls as one (PC), and what a list would bury opens as a sheet (PC › Apps). Ctrl+K finds every row in Exo, not only rooms and games, and lands on it lit for a moment.
