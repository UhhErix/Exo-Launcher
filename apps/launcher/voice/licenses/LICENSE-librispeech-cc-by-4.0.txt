LibriSpeech ASR Corpus Attribution
Dataset: LibriSpeech dev-clean (OpenSLR SLR12, https://www.openslr.org/12/)
Authors: Vassil Panayotov, Guoguo Chen, Daniel Povey, and Sanjeev Khudanpur,
"Librispeech: an ASR corpus based on public domain audio books,"
Proc. IEEE ICASSP 2015, pp. 5206-5210.

License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Usage in Exo Launcher:
500 clean speech utterances from LibriSpeech dev-clean (alongside 128 disjoint
DNS Challenge LibriVox public-domain clips and Exo MIT procedural gaming noise)
were used to fine-tune Exo Launcher's gaming noise suppression models
(exo_dpdfnet_baseline_gaming.onnx and exo_dpdfnet2_48khz_hr_gaming.onnx).
No raw LibriSpeech audio is redistributed inside Exo Launcher.
